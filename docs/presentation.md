# Fifteen-minute presentation plan

| Minutes | Content and evidence |
|---|---|
| 0–2 | Business risk, minimal app and incident declaration. Show pre-incident marker and start the approved recovery workflow early. |
| 2–5 | Architecture diagram, security boundaries, pilot-light tradeoff and provider choice. Explain that storage B is permanent and compute B is temporary. |
| 5–7 | Shared Terraform root, regional inputs/state keys, locking, moved addresses and saved-plan approval. |
| 7–9 | OIDC/IAM boundaries, secrets, private encrypted buckets, CI lint/security findings and documented exceptions. |
| 9–11 | Dashboard, actual alarm evidence, backup/replication proof, budget and actual-vs-estimated cost. |
| 11–14 | Recovery result, B URL and marker query. Show measured RTO/RPO artifact. Demonstrate or show clearly labeled recorded failback and teardown evidence. |
| 14–15 | Shared responsibility, known limitations and business conclusion. |

Record a complete real rehearsal with timestamps first. If live cold provisioning
does not finish in the presentation slot, show live status honestly and use the
labeled recording; do not invent success or replace actual timings with targets.
Bring the report, workflow URLs, recording link, calculator/billing export and
resource-removal evidence. Destroy demo compute after the presentation through
the approved workflow. No video or completed exercise is generated by this file.
